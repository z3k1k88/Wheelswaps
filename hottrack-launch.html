The Problem

eBay, Vinted & Co.
Weren't Built For Us

"I spent 45 minutes searching for a specific Car Culture release and couldn't find it because nobody listed it properly."

🔍
No collector-specific filters. eBay has no concept of tiers, series, sub-series or collector numbers. You can't search by the code on the back of your card. You're searching in the dark.
📝
Inconsistent listings. One seller writes "Super Treasure Hunt", another writes "STH", another writes "rare". It's chaos.
💸
Fees eat your profit. eBay charges sellers up to 15% plus PayPal fees. On a €10 car that's €1.50 gone before shipping.
🤝
No community. eBay is a transaction machine. There's no place for collectors to connect, ask questions or share their finds.
